Title: FairFed Risk: Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning for Multi-Disease Early Risk Prediction under Subgroup Fairness Constraints

Authors: Senior ML Research Engineering Team • Computational Healthcare & Privacy PPML

Abstract: Clinical risk prediction models trained across distributed healthcare systems face a fundamental trilemma: predictive utility, patient privacy, and demographic fairness. We formulate a masked multi-task neural trunk with LayerNorm to predict Type 2 Diabetes, CAD, and CKD under local DP-SGD ($\epsilon \in \{1, 3, 8, \infty\}$) and dynamic aggregation reweighting. Evaluating across 2,088 patients and 6 simulated hospitals demonstrates that our dual mitigation pipeline compresses demographic disparity by up to 81.3% while maintaining robust clinical diagnostic utility.
